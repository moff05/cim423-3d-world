This is the build log for my CIM 423 semester project at the University of Miami: one 3D world, developed across three assignments.

1. **Generate Your Story** — the narrative basis for the world
2. **Create a 3D World** — building the scene with primitives and free assets
3. **Adding Interactions** — five custom interaction points, including a start and end screen

See the [asset list](/ASSETS.html) for everything pulled in from Kenney.nl, Poly Haven, and Sketchfab.
